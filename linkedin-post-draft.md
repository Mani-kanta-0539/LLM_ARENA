## Option 1: Short & Punchy (Recommended)

🚀 Just built something cool — **LLM Arena**!

It's a platform where AI models like GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama, and Mistral go head-to-head in real-time.

**What is it?**
A web app with 5 interactive modes where you can watch different AI models debate, teach, roleplay, automate tasks, and work together as a team — all in one place.

**Why I built it:**
I wanted to compare how different LLMs think, argue, and create — not just benchmark scores, but real conversations side by side. Plus, I wanted to learn by building something big with Next.js, 3D environments, and multi-AI pipelines.

**The 5 modes:**
🎤 **Debate Room** — Two AIs argue any topic. You vote who wins.
📚 **Classroom** — AI teachers explain concepts in a 3D classroom. You ask doubts, they answer.
⚡ **Automation** — Research paper generator with a 4-node AI pipeline (Analyzer → Writer → Image Fetcher → Combiner). Export as PDF/DOCX.
🎭 **Play Mode** — "What if" scenarios. Make historical figures or sci-fi characters talk using different LLMs.
🤖 **AI Agents** — Multi-agent simulation. Give a project brief and watch AI agents (Designer, Developer, Marketing, Manager) collaborate on a Kanban board.

**Key features:**
✅ 12+ LLM models supported (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, DeepSeek, Groq, Cerebras)
✅ Bring your own API keys — works with any provider
✅ Pixel-art characters with speaking & walking animations
✅ 3D classroom environment (Three.js + WebGPU)
✅ Real-time streaming responses
✅ n8n-style workflow visualization for automations
✅ PDF & DOCX export for generated research papers
✅ Auth system with login/signup
✅ Fully responsive, dark-mode UI with neon aesthetics

**Tech stack:**
Next.js · TypeScript · Framer Motion · Three.js · Tailwind CSS · Pexels API · SSE Streaming · jsPDF · docx

Building this taught me a lot about multi-AI orchestration, streaming architectures, and making complex apps feel simple.

Would love your feedback! 💬
